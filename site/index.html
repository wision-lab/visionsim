Perception is
complementary.

One simulated scene, read by many sensor models. Blur, photon noise, event thresholds, phase wrapping and heat — applied to a world whose annotations never move.

01 Conventional RGB 40 ms exposure · motion blur

Fourteen annotations for every view.

Every view arrives with what the renderer already knew: how far each pixel is, which way the surface faces, where it moved from and to, what object it belongs to, what it is made of, where it sits in the world, how hot it is. Camera intrinsics and extrinsics are exact for every frame — nothing here is estimated.

Batteries included.

It comes with a large-scale dataset rendered through this pipeline: 100M raw data points from eight sensors, across 100 indoor scenes, with 480k synchronized ground-truth views. Coming soon — the prerelease is available in the meantime.
